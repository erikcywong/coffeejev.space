How to use the pack

Technical due diligence: send 01 + 02 + 03 together. Let them move the sliders and break a rule themselves — faster than any demo.

Commercial due diligence: send 04 + 05 + 06. Finance checks the three-party ledger, legal checks the compliance boundary, procurement checks the BOM.

Negotiating a share or a bond: attach the pre-registered pilot and shared-savings agreement, whose endpoints, sample size, randomisation unit and attribution rules are locked before the run.

What not to do: do not commit to these numbers as measurements; the baseline, the factors and the prices all await verification.
