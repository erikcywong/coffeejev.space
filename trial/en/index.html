01 1. Purpose and parties

This agreement compares, under the same nursery, the same batch lineage and the same environment, two regimes — "with the judgement layer" and "under current SOP" — and converts the difference into a shared-savings share by a recomputable method. Signature completes pre-registration: endpoints, sample size, randomisation unit and attribution rules are locked before the run and cannot be changed afterwards.

Why pre-registration is mandatory. If endpoints are chosen after the data are seen, any "improvement" can be explained into existence. The price of pre-registration is losing the freedom to adjust afterwards; what it buys is a result a third party can recompute.

04 4. Baseline freeze (co-signed)

The share is computed against baseline, so the baseline must be frozen before the run and may not be set by either party alone.

Rules for changing a frozen value: a revision may be requested only for (1) an error in the raw data or (2) a misapplied method; it needs the written consent of all three parties and re-registration; data already produced may not be recomputed retroactively.

05 5. The randomisation unit is the shelf tier, not the tray

press shelf because fogging, airflow and staff movement are highly correlated within a tier: trays on one tier affect each other, and tray-level randomisation lets the treatment's fogging and handling leak into the control arm.

The price is a smaller effective sample (design effect ≈ 1 + (m−1)·ICC, where m is trays per tier and ICC is taken as 0.15). Hence the target rises from 550 to 830 trays to reach 90% power; this agreement is signed at 830 trays per arm.

06 6. Attribution and the share

Attribution rests on two things only: the baseline freeze (clause 04) and the randomised arms (clause 05). Environmental covariates (temperature, light, power price, season) are listed as adjustments but never used to explain a difference away.

07 7. Data sharing and access

  • Raw data belongs to the site. Party B processes it only within the authorised scope; the verifier may read it for verification but may not retain copies.
  • De-identified aggregate data may be used for method research and industry benchmarks only if the box is ticked at signature (Appendix B).
  • No party may selectively disclose interim results; all endpoint data are released to the three parties at once in stage D.
  • Data from device failure, calibration drift or connectivity loss is marked "degraded" and excluded from the primary endpoint.

08 8. Suspension and exit

  • Safety stop: on a suspected pathogen or an irreversible disposal need, stage B stops at once; the site decides the response and the data are kept.
  • Validity stop: if shadow-phase agreement is <0.85 the trial may not enter B; the agreement terminates with no share.
  • Withdrawal: 30 days' written notice; data already produced are still handled under clause 07, and fees already incurred are not refunded.
