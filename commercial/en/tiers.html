02 / PRODUCT LINES

Three product lines

Three lines: grower pays judgement, buyer pays evidence

The first line is sold to the grower as a cost item; the second and third go to the buyer as compliance and premium items that can be passed downstream. All three share one metering setup, one ledger and one dataset — the marginal cost is incurred once, on the first line.

How one batch is paid for twice: The grower pays ¥3,450–8,850 for "money not lost"; the buyer pays ¥35,000–70,000 for the labels and bonds that make the crop sellable. The two lines share no payer, only the same ledger over the same batch — which is why label fees can balance the books for a small site.
