CoffeeJeV
EUDR · export compliance gate

A container of coffee must prove it is not from deforested land

EUDR does not ask for a handsome ESG report; it asks for an evidence chain that lands on coordinates. Our part is narrow: link batches, plots and the ledger into recomputable release material. Our non-part is just as explicit: we do not decide deforestation and we do not issue the due-diligence statement.

Draw the boundary first. This page describes a product design, not a live service. At the time of writing the verifier is not contracted, there is no plot-data interface, and the regulatory timetable is still moving. Every price and ratio here is a design anchor, pending measurement and legal review.

Timetable: under the Commission's late-2024 postponement, large operators from 2025-12-30 and SMEs from 2026-06-30; simplification and further delay are still under discussion. The EUR-Lex text governs; this page does not restate dates.

02 What we do, and what we do not

What we deliver (existing capability, one layer of packaging)

  • Batch-to-plot binding: the plot_id and its polygon go into the batch manifest and are hashed with the label
  • An immutable transport chain: batch id → container id → bill of lading, each hop written to the hash-chained ledger
  • Release evidence pack: one per container, holding plot coordinates, the batch ledger digest, the PCF label and the review record
  • Public verification: anyone can recompute it from the container id and the first 12 hash digits in a browser, without our servers
  • Field mapping aligned to the DDS: our fields are mapped to the keys an importer needs when filing, so nothing is typed twice

What we do not do (the parts most easily misread)

  • We do not decide deforestation: the deforestation-free conclusion rests on the operator's due diligence and satellite evidence; we only fix the evidence in place
  • We do not issue the DDS: the DDS is the EU importer's legal responsibility, not the supplier's
  • We do not stand in for certification: no accreditation body has certified us, and we do not claim equivalence with EUDR compliance
  • We do not survey plots: coordinates come from the grower or cooperative; we validate format and consistency, we do not produce them
  • We do not guarantee customs clearance: the evidence chain lowers the risk of refusal; it does not displace the authority's judgement

03 The data model: a chain that can land

plot_id + polygon→ batch_id→ harvest window→ container_id→ DDS ref

Each hop takes the previous hash as input, the same fnv(prev ‖ canonical(payload)) used by the existing label chain. The only difference is three extra fields in the payload:

FieldSourceWho may change it
plot_polygonprovided by the grower (GeoJSON, WGS84, at least 6 decimal places)immutable; a change ships a new version and splits the batch
container_id / B/Lthe carrier's bill of ladingimmutable; one container, one hash
dds_refobtained by the importer in the EU systemmay be filled in once, then frozen
Known gaps — written here rather than hidden. (1) there is no plot-data interface yet; plot_polygon exists only in the design; (2) no satellite baseline is connected, so forest cover at 2020-12-31 cannot be checked automatically; (3) the four legality document classes need item-by-item review by local counsel, and we only archive and hash them. Until those three are solved, this page is an interface design.

Why per container and not per batch: EUDR enforcement acts on the cargo, and clearance happens at container level. Per-batch billing would split one container into a dozen billing points and add friction. This is the same conclusion both the site review and the pricing audit reached: the fee has to follow the cargo.

05 Why this sells before the carbon label does

The need is binary, not a premium

A carbon label sells a better price; EUDR sells the right to enter. The first can be postponed, the second cannot.

The payer is the importer, not the grower

Without a DDS the importer cannot release the goods; it is a compliance cost, spread across cargo value. The grower only supplies coordinates.

It reuses the ledger; no new system

Batch lineage, the hash chain and the metering record already run; the only additions are plot coordinates and the container hop.

It bundles with the carbon label

One container, one evidence pack: it answers both whether the goods are compliant and what their footprint is. Collect once, bill twice.

In one line. EUDR does not lack reports; it lacks a way for a third party to recompute one container's coordinates, batch and transport record. That is exactly what this ledger already does — swap carbon for plots and container ids and the structure holds.
