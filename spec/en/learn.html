07 / LEARNING LOOP

Learning loop

Labels arrive late, so the loop is designed to be asynchronous

This is the section most easily skipped, and the one most likely to leave the system dead in six months. The idea: do not try to make the model learn in real time; instead make every judgement leave a settleable receipt and settle in batches once the labels arrive.

7.1 Five-step closed loop

① Settlement 7–45 days

Decision events come with label_ready_at. It takes 14 days for contamination, 21 days for rooting, and 45 days for transplant survival. At the end of the period, the final label will be backfilled by a scheduled task.

Before there is a final label, the sample is labeled pending, Not participating in training- This is the gate that prevents label noise from contaminating the calibration.

② Calibration weekly

0.05 triggers refitting.

③ Drift daily

Three levels: input drift (PSI/KL on sensor and image features), prediction drift (decision distribution), and result drift (contamination rate/survival rate). The first two are visible the same day, the third will have to wait 45 days.

④ Shadow ≥30 days

The new model runs in parallel with the new farm, only recording but not executing. Compare the consistency rate and disagreement sample distribution of AI and artificial intelligence, Disagreement is not a mistake, it is a message.

⑤ Promotion quarter

Threshold: Cost-weighted net income does not decrease And ECE does not rise And The review consistency rate does not decrease. If any of the three are not satisfied, it will not go online - even if the accuracy is higher.

active learning queue

Priority is given to labeling three types of samples: Review Area Disagreement (Both the model and the person hesitate), OOD hit、high confidence error. The amount of information for each 100 pieces of these three categories is approximately equal to 1,000 random pieces.

7.2 Calibration: Why 0.82 cannot be used directly The curved line on the reliability chart is the difference between "confidence" and "correctness"

ideal diagonal Uncalibrated (overconfidence) After calibration

Three actions, from low to high cost

  • temperature scaling (cheapest): p' = σ(logit(p)/T), a scalar parameter that barely changes the ranking, only the credibility scale.
  • Isotonic / binning calibration 2000); small samples are prone to overfitting.
  • Conformal abstention: Without correcting the probability, directly give "how big is the answer set of this sample". Collection contains ≥2 categories → waive upgrade. This is the only method with a limited sample guarantee.

In engineering, there are three superpositions: temperature scaling tube scale, conformal tube boundary, and OOD tube. "This set of scales is not applicable here at all."

Note: The threshold cannot be calculated by cost alone. The pure cost solution will give an answer of 4% "almost every plate is isolated" - there must be lower operating limit (the amount of false isolation that can be tolerated per week) brings it up, which is why τ_cost is shown separately from τ_hi in panel A.
