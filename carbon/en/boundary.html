02 / BOUNDARY & FUNCTIONAL UNIT

Boundary and functional unit: what is included, and why each exclusion holds

cradle-to-gate · ISO 14067 / PAS 2050 aligned · GHG Protocol scopes

The system boundary is cradle-to-gate: from upstream input production and nursery operation through delivery of the seedling at the gate (gate to gate, including outbound transport). It excludes the grow-out stage on the farm, consumer use and final disposal. Scope 1 / 2 / 3 follow the GHG Protocol, and every exclusion carries an auditable reason rather than "not counted for now".

Land-use change (LUC)Scope 3Excluded—The nursery sits on existing agricultural land, so no LUC occurs; expanding onto forest land would require reassessment. Peat extraction on the baseline side falls outside this boundary, see §6
Biogenic CO2 (photosynthetic uptake)—Excluded—Under ISO 14067, biogenic uptake and short-term storage are not part of the product footprint and may not offset anything. A seedling is not a carbon sink and may not be marketed as one
Staff commuting / office / travelScope 3Excluded—<1% and physically unrelated to the product, excluded under the cut-off rule; marketing may not use this to claim a "complete boundary"

Cut-off rule: a source below 1% of the total, and below 5% in aggregate, may be excluded (PAS 2050 practice). Exclusions here total about 1.5%; Scope 1 is not quantified but is a data gap rather than a cut-off decision, and the maintenance ledger must be completed before verification.
